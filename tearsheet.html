1. The business

Rentnito gives landlords and tenants a signed, timestamped record of a unit's condition at move-in and move-out, so deposit disagreements can be settled against evidence. Around that core sit a move-in checklist that connects tenants to movers, internet and energy providers, an overseas video walkthrough for people renting a unit they have not seen, a landlord compliance checklist (HDB, IRAS, URA duties), a tiered dispute process, and a repair request log with automatic reminders and escalation for unresponsive landlords. The core product is record keeping, so it avoids property agency and payment licensing.

How the numbers are built.

  • Revenue multiple of 2x to 4x. This is an assumption for a small, profitable software and marketplace business in one market, not a benchmark taken from named transactions. It sits at the low end because the business is single-market, small and unproven. It should be checked against comparable deals before any equity discussion.
  • Value today. Year 5 value divided by (1 + discount rate) to the power of 5. The low end uses 2x and a 60% annual discount rate (divisor about 10.5). The high end uses 4x and 40% (divisor about 5.4). Rates this high reflect pre-launch execution risk, untested willingness to pay and a solo team.
  • Cross-check. Base case Year 5 operating profit of S$148k on S$336k revenue means the multiples above equal roughly 4.5x to 9x operating profit, which is modest for a profitable software business.
  • What would move the range. Evidence that landlords pay (pre-sales and renewals), signed partner agreements, a repeatable low-cost acquisition channel, and a second market. Singapore alone supports a small profitable business, so a larger outcome depends on those levers.

6. Key risks

  • Willingness to pay is untested; pre-sell before building much.
  • Tenant-side cold start; free tier and link-based sharing.
  • Existing platforms such as Rently are fuller marketplaces; a full competitor scan is still to do.
  • Deposit holding likely needs MAS licensing; legal advice first.
  • PDPA applies to photos and personal data; minimal data, no NRIC.

7. What we are looking for

  • A partnerships lead (movers, ISPs, energy retailers, cleaners).
  • Growth and content (SEO, community outreach, landlord acquisition).
  • Design and front end (mobile-first, no app download).
  • Open to equity, revenue share or milestone-based arrangements.
